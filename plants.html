Plant guide

Meet the plant friends and the plant bullies of Southern California.

Word wizard

Native

A plant that has grown in a place for a very long time, without people bringing it there.

Invasive

A plant from somewhere else that spreads fast and crowds out plants that belong.

Habitat

The kind of place where a plant or animal lives, like a beach, a forest, or a river.

Restoration

Helping a damaged place grow back to the way nature made it.

Meet the habitats

Coastal sage scrub

Dry, foggy places near the coast. Short shrubs that smell good. Home to birds like the coastal California gnatcatcher.

Chaparral

Steep hills covered with tightly packed shrubs.

Oak & walnut woodland

Shady places with big oak and walnut trees.

Wetlands & rivers

Wet places with willows, cottonwoods, and cattails. There used to be a lot more of them.

Meadows & grasslands

Open, sunny places with wildflowers and grasses.
