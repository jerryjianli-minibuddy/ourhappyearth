WHERE can we help?

Let’s start close to home and think big. A satellite map lets us look down on Earth like a bird!

Satellite map

Zoom in and look around. Green places have lots of plants. Brown places are dry or bare. Gray places have buildings and roads. Tap a fire to learn about it.

Fires:
  • New fire (started in the last two weeks)
  • Burn scar (burned earlier this year, now healing)
  • Famous past fire (big fire from an earlier year)
  • Our school (El Rincon Elementary, home of the Rockets)
  • Bigger mark = bigger fire.

Looking for fires…

Click a flame or a burned area to meet the animals that might live there and learn how fire changes their home.

Make your own pin: Click or tap anywhere on the map to mark a place you want to study. Your pins stay on this computer.

Is it a good spot? Use the checklist!

Scientists ask questions before they plant. Check each one off as you think about it.

Ask an expert! A plant expert can tell us which plants and places are best. Native plant nurseries, nature centers, and park rangers love to help kids.
