Plant friends

Native plants have lived here for a very long time. Birds, bees, and butterflies know them and need them. They are plant friends!

Plant bullies

Invasive plants come from somewhere else. They grow super fast and crowd out the plant friends. Some can even make wildfires worse.

Play Bully Buster!

Safety first! Always ask a grown-up before you plant, pull, or touch plants outside. Never taste a plant you find in the wild.
